# Attribution

These files arrive with `fetch-assets`. They are not committed.

## Plates and collage assets

Historic plates, paper textures, perches, and label fonts come from
[Fugleramme](https://github.com/arnegiacomo/fugleramme) by Arne Giacomo
Munthe-Kaas. Artwork is [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Fonts are SIL OFL 1.1; see `assets/fonts/ATTRIBUTION.md` after fetch.

## Bird body masses

`bird_sizes.csv` is from AVONET (Tobias et al. 2022, Ecology Letters,
doi:10.1111/ele.13898), CC BY 4.0.

## Name aliases

`birdnet_aliases.json` is the compiled alias map from
[OpenFauna](https://github.com/tphakala/openfauna) by tphakala, CC BY-SA 4.0.
